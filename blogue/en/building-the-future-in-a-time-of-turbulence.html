Building the Future in a Time of Turbulence

Jean-François Lavigne February 26, 2025 4 min read

English version of a piece originally published in French. Read the French original.

The Canadian economy is entering a period of turbulence. Discussions about productivity and automation dominate, but these solutions require time and significant investments. A more immediate and strategic approach is needed: leveraging our collective talent by aligning the right leaders with the right roles. By capitalizing on human capital, we can navigate uncertainty and build stronger, more resilient organizations.

Québec is rich in talent. The absolute priority must be placing the right people in the right seats. As Jim Collins states in Good to Great: first who, then what.

But this transformation cannot be the responsibility of one company or individual alone. Québec's entire economic ecosystem must evolve. Businesses, executives, recruiters, and investors have a shared responsibility: to rethink their practices and work together to put human capital at the heart of our economic strategies. The opportunity is real. It's up to us to seize it.

Maximizing Talent: A Growth Engine

A high-performance engine needs the right fuel. When talent is well-positioned, it becomes a key differentiator. Too often, executive recruitment is reduced to filling vacant positions reactively. However, anticipation and precision turn recruitment into a powerful lever for value creation.

Imagine a Québec where every talent is in the right place. An economy where every company, from SMEs to major corporations, benefits from leaders who optimize strengths and drive innovation. The impact would be tremendous: increased productivity, enhanced competitiveness, and amplified collective prosperity. This vision is within our reach if we commit to fully investing in our human capital.

Taking the Time to Get It Right

In a world that moves faster every day, it is easy to fall into the trap of urgency. However, aligning talent cannot be done under pressure. Building a strong leadership team requires strategic thinking that extends beyond immediate needs. Every executive appointment must fit into a long-term vision, reinforcing the organization's stability and growth. A wise CEO takes the time needed to align their leadership team with their vision. It's not about slowing down but about structuring actions with discipline and precision.

A Thoughtful Approach to Recruitment

Building a strong leadership team is an essential responsibility, not a lottery. The process follows the principles of Planning, Organizing, Directing, and Controlling (PODC), with a critical feedback loop. Well-prepared companies establish a clear succession plan, ensuring strategic continuity and long-term resilience. This is a major issue, particularly for our SMEs.

From a recruitment perspective, deep management expertise and an intimate understanding of organizations are essential. Recruiting effectively isn't about filling a vacancy, it's about identifying leaders who will integrate into a team, transform the company, and positively influence its future.

The Chemistry Between Leader and Organization

Recruiting an executive is about more than just filling a role, it's about creating synergy between an individual and a complex ecosystem. It requires:

  • Understanding the organizational structure and internal dynamics.
  • Decoding culture and core values to anticipate decision-making.
  • Anticipating market trends to position the company strategically.
  • Assessing financial health and internal systems to sustain growth.
  • Aligning leadership with strategic vision to ensure long-term impact.

Assisted by their closest advisors, the CEO must be directly involved at every step of the process. Refocusing on strategy rather than treating recruitment as a transactional process strengthens the link between leadership and vision. These two elements drive execution. Everything starts with the right leader in the right place. Without that, any vision remains theoretical.

We Have the Talent, Let's Align It Properly

Proper talent alignment creates a level of collective intelligence that becomes a powerful success factor and a distinct competitive advantage. Aligning talent must be the ultimate priority for Québec's CEOs. Before anything else, businesses must ensure the right leaders are in the right positions. Every decision must be carefully considered, every talent placed with precision to maximize impact.

Talent alignment is just the beginning. It raises deeper questions: how can we better assess and develop talent? How do we recognize and promote leadership? How do we structure compensation to drive real performance? On these issues, I leave the floor to other management experts to expand the conversation.

Talent first. Everything else follows.

Faced with the turbulence caused by U.S. protectionism, Québec must double down on its human capital. Our businesses have the power to adapt and thrive by investing in talent and strengthening resilience. Now is the time to act with vision and boldness.

Good to Great.
